A bit about me.

Background

I'm a final-year linguistics student at Magdalene College, Cambridge. In second year I took papers on computational linguistics, syntax, psycholinguistics and typology; this year I'm studying neuroscience and language acquisition, with a dissertation testing the validity of a proposed universal syntactic constraint on contact varieties like Malaysian English and Baba Malay.

Outside my academics, I am active in both college and university life by running a few societies. I'm currently learning Arabic and self-studying Spanish, on top of exploring blind wine-tasting as a hobby and challenging myself for half-marathon running. I am a fortunate scholar under the generous YH2 Capital Scholarship, and was born and raised in Malaysia.
